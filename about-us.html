Our story

About LAVIGO

We are Himalayan travel specialists who design tailor-made journeys across Sikkim, Darjeeling, Himachal and Nepal.

Who we are

The Himalaya, slowly and comfortably.

LAVIGO was born from a simple belief: the Himalaya should be experienced slowly, comfortably and with people who know it well. We plan journeys to the mountains we love - from the monasteries of Gangtok and the tea gardens of Darjeeling to the high lakes of North Sikkim, the valleys of Manali and the temple towns of Nepal.

What we do

Every LAVIGO trip is built around you. Tell us your dates, your travel style and what you dream of seeing, and we design a day-by-day itinerary with handpicked stays, experienced mountain drivers and the right amount of time in each place.

Why travel with us

Local knowledge

we know which viewpoints clear first in the morning, which roads close after rain and where to find the best momos in town.

Permits handled

North Sikkim, Tsomgo Lake, Nathu La and Rohtang Pass all need permits. We take care of the paperwork so you can focus on the journey.

Comfort at altitude

we pace your trip to help you acclimatise, choose warm, comfortable stays and always build in buffer time for mountain weather.

Real support

one dedicated contact before and throughout your trip, just a call or message away.

Who we travel with

Families, couples and honeymooners, friends, solo travellers and small groups. Whether it is your first trip to the mountains or your tenth, we will make it feel effortless.
