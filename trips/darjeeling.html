Darjeeling: Queen of the Hills

Sunrise over Kanchenjunga, a ride on the UNESCO-listed toy train and the world-famous tea gardens.

Itinerary

Day-by-day plan

  1. Day1

    Arrive – drive to Darjeeling

    Pick-up from Bagdogra Airport or NJP station and a winding drive through tea estates to Darjeeling (approx. 3 hours). Evening at leisure on the Mall and Chowrasta.

  2. Day2

    Tiger Hill sunrise

    An early start to Tiger Hill to watch the first light strike Kanchenjunga, the world's third-highest peak. Return via Ghoom Monastery and the Batasia Loop war memorial.

  3. Day3

    Toy train & mountain heritage

    Ride the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, then visit the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute, Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park and the Japanese Peace Pagoda.

  4. Day4

    Tea gardens & ropeway

    Tour a working tea estate with a tasting of first and second flush Darjeeling teas, then glide over the valley on the Darjeeling ropeway.

  5. Day5

    Journey home

    Transfer back to Bagdogra or NJP, or continue onward to Gangtok and Sikkim.
