Manali & the Kullu Valley

Pine-forested valleys, snow-capped passes, adventure sports and the timeless temples of the Kullu Valley.

Itinerary

Day-by-day plan

  1. Day1

    Arrive Manali

    Arrive by flight to Bhuntar (Kullu) or by overnight coach from Delhi. Check in to your hotel and relax with views of the Beas river and the surrounding peaks.

  2. Day2

    Temples & Old Manali

    Visit the cedar-wood Hadimba Devi Temple, the hot springs of Vashisht and Manu Temple, then explore the cafés and lanes of Old Manali.

  3. Day3

    Solang Valley

    A day of mountain adventure at Solang Valley – ropeway rides, paragliding, ziplining and, in winter, skiing and snow activities.

  4. Day4

    Atal Tunnel & Rohtang Pass

    Drive through the Atal Tunnel to Sissu in the Lahaul valley, or up to Rohtang Pass (13,050 ft) in season. Rohtang permits are limited and arranged in advance.

  5. Day5

    Naggar & Kullu

    Visit the historic Naggar Castle and the Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery, with optional river rafting on the Beas and shopping for Kullu shawls.

  6. Day6

    Journey home

    Transfer to Bhuntar Airport or the coach stand for your onward journey.
