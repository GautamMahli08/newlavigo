Itinerary

Day-by-day plan

  1. Day1

    Gangtok to Lachen

    Depart Gangtok for North Sikkim, passing the Seven Sisters Waterfall, Naga Falls and the river confluence at Chungthang, arriving in the alpine village of Lachen by evening (approx. 6–7 hours).

  2. Day2

    Gurudongmar Lake & on to Lachung

    A pre-dawn start for Gurudongmar Lake, one of the highest lakes in the world at about 17,800 ft and sacred to Buddhists and Sikhs alike. Visit Chopta Valley on the return, then continue to Lachung.

  3. Day3

    Yumthang Valley & Zero Point

    Explore the Yumthang "Valley of Flowers", carpeted with rhododendrons and primulas in spring, and its natural hot springs. Optional excursion to Zero Point (Yumesamdong) and the Lachung Monastery.

  4. Day4

    Return to Gangtok

    Drive back to Gangtok with stops along the Teesta valley, for a final evening on MG Marg.
