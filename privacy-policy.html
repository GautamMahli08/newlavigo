Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

LAVIGO plans Himalayan trips to Sikkim, Darjeeling, Himachal Pradesh and Nepal. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you fill in our trip request form or message us on WhatsApp, phone or email, we collect the details you share: your name, phone number, email address, travel dates, number and ages of travellers, budget and trip preferences.

To book permits and stays we may also ask for identity documents and the names and ages of everyone travelling.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, design your itinerary, confirm prices and availability, and manage your booking.

To arrange hotels, drivers, permits and other services that are part of your trip.

To contact you about your trip before, during and after you travel.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with the hotels, transport providers and permit offices involved in your trip, and with authorities when the law requires it. We never sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking details for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at info@YOURDOMAIN.com or on WhatsApp at +91 90314 74036.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
