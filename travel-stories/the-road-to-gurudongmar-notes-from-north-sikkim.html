The Road to Gurudongmar: Notes from North Sikkim

Waterfalls, yak pastures and a sacred lake at 17,800 feet - the most spectacular road trip in the eastern Himalaya.

Lachen & Lachung 2 min read

The road north from Gangtok follows the Teesta river into ever-deeper gorges. Waterfalls tumble straight onto the tarmac, prayer flags are strung across suspension bridges, and every bend seems to reveal a higher, whiter peak.

A night in Lachen

By evening you reach Lachen, a village of wooden houses at around 2,750 metres. Dinner is simple and warming - thukpa, momos and butter tea - and bedtime is early, because the drive to Gurudongmar starts long before sunrise.

Gurudongmar Lake

The road climbs above the tree line into a high, open plateau close to the Tibetan border. Then the lake appears: impossibly blue, ringed by snow peaks and sacred to both Buddhists and Sikhs. At over 5,000 metres the air is thin, so visits are kept short - but they stay with you for a lifetime.

Yumthang, the Valley of Flowers

From Lachung, a morning drive leads into the Yumthang Valley. In late spring it is carpeted with rhododendrons and primulas; yaks graze along the river and natural hot springs steam by the roadside.

Good to know

North Sikkim needs a Protected Area Permit, which we arrange in advance. Gurudongmar Lake is currently open to Indian nationals only, and roads can close after heavy snow or rain - so we always build a little flexibility into the plan.
