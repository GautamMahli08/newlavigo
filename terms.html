About these terms

These terms apply when you book a trip with LAVIGO. Please read them before you confirm a booking. Your personalised quote may include additional terms for your specific trip.

Prices

Prices shown on this website are per person, in Indian Rupees, and are estimates. Travellers above 10 years pay the full trip price, children aged 3 to 10 pay half price and children under 3 travel free (sharing existing beds and seats).

Your final price is confirmed in writing (usually on WhatsApp or email) before you pay and may vary with travel dates, hotel availability and group size.

Booking and payment

A booking is confirmed once you accept our quote and pay the advance stated in it. The balance is due before your trip starts, as set out in your quote.

Changes and cancellations

If you need to change or cancel your trip, contact us as early as possible. Cancellation charges depend on how close to the start date you cancel and on the policies of the hotels and services booked for you; they will be listed in your quote.

Permits and documents

Some areas, including North Sikkim, Tsomgo Lake, Nathu La and Rohtang Pass, require permits. We arrange these for you, but you must provide valid identity documents on time. Permits can be refused or restricted by the authorities, and some areas are closed to foreign nationals.

Weather and road conditions

Mountain weather, landslides and road closures can change plans at short notice. We will always try to offer the best alternative, but we cannot be responsible for delays or changes caused by events outside our control. Any extra costs from such events are payable by the traveller.

Health and safety

High-altitude travel can affect anyone. Please tell us about any medical conditions before booking, follow the advice of your driver and guide, and consider travel insurance that covers high-altitude trips.
